Questions

Answered before you ask.

How can it be ten days?

Because scope is fixed before work begins and we don't run projects in parallel. Most of a conventional agency timeline is waiting on approvals, not designing.

Can I edit the site myself afterwards?

Yes, on the full site and bespoke tiers. You get a proper editor, a handover document and a short recorded walkthrough.

Who writes the copy?

We draft it, you approve it. You supply the facts about your business; we handle order, length and emphasis.

What happens if I don't like the first draft?

Two revision rounds are included and we'd rather you said so plainly. If it's still not right after those, you don't pay the balance and you keep the files.

Do I own everything?

Completely — domain, code and content. No lock-in, no hostage hosting, and you can take it elsewhere any time.

Do you handle SEO?

The technical foundations, yes: structure, speed, metadata and schema. Ongoing content campaigns aren't something we take on.

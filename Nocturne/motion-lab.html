What I'd actually build

Six free effects, placed deliberately. Then decide on 3D.

Hero

Keep the type reveal, add scroll-velocity weight (07). The hero's job is to be read in five seconds. Anything heavier fights that.

Selected work

Horizontal gallery (03) with cursor-carried previews (05). These two together are the strongest pairing here — the work becomes the centrepiece without the page getting longer.

Manifesto line

Scroll-focused text (01). One sentence, revealed at reading pace, somewhere between the work and the pricing.

Process

Pinned sequence (02). Your three steps are already written for this. It's the most cinematic thing you can do for free.

Pricing

CSS 3D tilt (09) on the featured tier only. Tilting all three flattens the hierarchy you're trying to create.

Images

Unmasking reveal (04) everywhere, as the default. Invisible when it's working.

On WebGL

I'd hold off, and here's the honest reason. Your entire pitch is fast, clean sites shipped in ten days. A hero that costs a megabyte contradicts it, and every prospect on a phone pays that toll. If you want real 3D later, the case for it is a spinning render of an actual client site — something that shows the work. Abstract shapes are decoration, and decoration is what everyone else is doing.
